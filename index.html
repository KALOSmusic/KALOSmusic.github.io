WHO IS KALOS

An independent artistic project — a space of research across music, narrative, visual identity and fictional artists.

KALOS is primarily focused on experimental music, avant-garde approaches and cross-genre exploration, while remaining open to music in many different forms and traditions. Projects can move from funk and rock to extreme metal, from jazz and traditional music to electronic experimentation, or toward softer and more atmospheric sounds.

The work is driven by a broad interest in music and by the idea that genre does not have to be a creative boundary. Each project can develop its own sound, identity and aesthetic, depending on the idea behind it. A project can begin with a musical intuition, a character, a fictional artist or band, a narrative concept, or an entire imagined world.

The work then develops through writing, experimentation, artistic direction, AI-assisted music creation, selection and post-production. AI-based tools are used as instruments within the creative process, alongside human decisions, editing and artistic direction.

Un progetto artistico indipendente, uno spazio di ricerca tra musica, narrazione, identità visiva e artisti immaginari.

Il cuore del progetto è la sperimentazione: approcci d'avanguardia, contaminazioni tra generi, la libertà di muoversi senza etichette fisse — pur restando aperto a ogni forma e tradizione musicale. Un progetto può nascere nel funk o nel rock, spingersi fino al metal più estremo, attingere al jazz o alla musica tradizionale, perdersi nell'elettronica sperimentale, oppure cercare atmosfere più morbide e sospese.

Alla base c'è la convinzione che il genere non sia un confine, ma solo un punto di partenza: ogni progetto costruisce il proprio suono, la propria identità, la propria estetica, a seconda dell'idea che lo mette in moto. Può nascere da un'intuizione musicale, da un personaggio, da un artista o una band immaginari, da un concept narrativo, o da un intero mondo da inventare.

Da lì, il lavoro prende forma attraverso scrittura, sperimentazione, direzione artistica, creazione musicale assistita dall'intelligenza artificiale, selezione e post-produzione — strumenti IA usati come parte del processo creativo, sempre dentro decisioni, scelte ed editing umani.

Collaborations & commissions.

KALOS is available for selected commissions and collaborative projects involving original music, sound and musical direction. This can include soundtracks and original music for films, video games, documentaries, visual art, installations, advertising and other creative projects.

Projects can be approached from different musical directions, from experimental and avant-garde work to more accessible or genre-based music, depending on the needs and identity of the project.

Each collaboration is developed around the specific project, with attention to its atmosphere, narrative, visual identity and overall artistic direction.

The KALOS website (kalosmusic.github.io) is an independent artistic project managed by Calogero Scafidi, as data controller for personal data processed through the website.

1. What data is processed

The website does not require registration, does not provide user accounts, does not use newsletters, advertising systems, or behavioural analytics tools.

The website may process:

  • technical data necessary for the operation and security of the hosting infrastructure;
  • personal data voluntarily included in messages sent to the project's contact email address;
  • technical data that may be processed by external services used to provide certain website functions, such as the YouTube player.

2. Purposes of processing

Any data processed may be used solely to:

  • ensure the proper operation and security of the website;
  • respond to communications voluntarily sent by users;
  • make the musical and audiovisual content available through the website.

The website does not use users' data for advertising or profiling purposes.

3. Third-party services

The website uses YouTube to provide playback of musical content.

Embedded players use, where technically possible, the youtube-nocookie.com domain (YouTube's Privacy Enhanced mode). Interaction with the player may nevertheless involve processing by YouTube in accordance with Google's policies.

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting service may process technical connection and access data for purposes related to the operation, security and management of its infrastructure.

Third-party services are subject to their respective privacy policies.

4. Email communications

If a user voluntarily contacts KALOS through the email address published on the website, the data contained in the communication will be used solely to manage and respond to the request.

Such data will not be used for marketing purposes without an appropriate legal basis or consent, where required.

5. Data retention

Data received by email will be retained for as long as necessary to manage the communication and any resulting relationship, and subsequently deleted when no further legal or operational reason justifies its retention.

Technical data processed by hosting and third-party services is subject to the retention periods and criteria established by the respective providers.

6. Data subject rights

Where provided for by applicable law, data subjects may exercise the rights recognised by data protection legislation, including the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection, as well as any other rights provided by the GDPR.

For questions concerning privacy or to exercise applicable rights, contact:
